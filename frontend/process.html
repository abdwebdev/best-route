Step-by-Step Excellence

Our Proven 7-Stage Relocation Workflow

From initial video survey to final television mounting and box disposal, here is how we ensure zero stress on moving day.

01

Free Pre-Move Survey & Written Quote

Our manager conducts an instant WhatsApp video survey or on-site visit to count boxes, check elevator access, and evaluate heavy items. You receive a fixed binding written quote.

✓ Timeline: Day -7 to Day -2
02

Building Permit (NOC) & Parking Management

We provide our trade license and worker Emirates IDs to building management (Emaar, Nakheel, DAMAC, DDA) so security guards grant seamless truck access.

✓ Timeline: Day -1
03

Export-Grade Packing & Color Tagging

Our packing crew arrives with heavy-duty boxes, wardrobe cartons, bubble wrap, and tissue paper. Every box is labeled with its target room.

✓ Timeline: Moving Day Morning (08:00 AM)
04

Carpenter Furniture Dismantling

Certified master carpenters carefully dismantle bed frames, IKEA wardrobes, dining tables, and TV wall brackets while organizing all screws in labeled bags.

✓ Timeline: Moving Day Morning (09:30 AM)
05

Enclosed Padded Truck Transport with Live GPS

Items are strapped inside air-ride enclosed trucks. You receive a direct WhatsApp tracking link to follow our truck live in transit.

✓ Timeline: Moving Day Midday
06

Unloading & Furniture Re-Assembly

Boxes are carried to designated rooms. Carpenters rebuild wardrobes, level bed frames, unhook appliances, and mount curtain rods.

✓ Timeline: Moving Day Afternoon
07

Debris Haul-Away & Final Quality Audit

We collect all used cardboard boxes, plastic bubble wrap, and tape for recycling. You inspect every room with our manager before final sign-off.

✓ Timeline: Completion Guarantee
